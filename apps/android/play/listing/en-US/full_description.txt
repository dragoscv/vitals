Vitals shows what your phone, your watch, your TV and your PC are really doing, in words a person can read, and never invents a number it could not measure.

THIS PHONE
• Processor load per core cluster, clock speeds and temperature
• Graphics load and clock where the phone reports them
• Memory in use and how close Android is to closing apps
• Battery: charge, health, temperature, voltage, current and charge cycles
• Storage: a map of what fills your phone, the largest folders and files, and a cleanup you review before anything is deleted
• Apps: time on screen and data used by each app over the last 24 hours
• Every sensor the phone has, live
• A week of history and alerts when something runs hot, low or full

YOUR WATCH
The Wear OS app monitors the watch itself and shows your PCs on your wrist, with a tile and complications for a PC's processor load and temperature.

YOUR TV
The Android TV app is built for the remote: it monitors the TV itself and shows your PCs on the big screen. Pair a PC by typing the six-digit code it shows, no camera needed.

YOUR PC (optional)
Pair a Windows PC running Vitals by scanning its QR code. See its processor, graphics, memory, temperatures and processes, get alerts, wake it from sleep, and with a control pairing end or suspend a process that has stopped responding. Two home screen widgets keep one PC in view. Keep several PCs in one list. Everything stays on your local network.

Vitals for Windows is free at vitals.dragoscatalin.ro. Remote access is off on the PC until you turn it on.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• If a reading cannot be measured, Vitals shows a dash, never a made-up zero
• No ads, no account, no tracking; the only diagnostics are Google ML Kit's own, when you scan a QR code
• Usage access and all-files access are optional; each feature that needs one says so and stays empty until you grant it
• Free and open source (MIT): github.com/dragoscv/vitals
