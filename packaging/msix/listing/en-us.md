## Description

Is your PC slow? Vitals tells you why, in plain words.

Press one button and Vitals looks at what is using your processor, memory, disk and network right now, and names the program responsible — "Chrome is using most of your memory", not a wall of numbers.

Everything Task Manager does, made clearer:
• Processes with their real app names and icons, grouped and sorted by what matters
• End a program, change its priority or open its folder with a right-click
• Startup apps with their real impact on how long Windows takes to start
• Performance graphs for processor, memory, disk, network and graphics card
• Installed apps, services, users, network connections and devices in one place

And things Task Manager never did:
• "Why is my PC slow?" — a plain-language answer from live readings
• Storage clean-up that shows what is safe to remove before removing anything
• A lag watchdog that offers to restart Explorer when Windows freezes
• A configurable dashboard, light and dark themes, ten accent colours
• An optional phone view of your PC on your home network (off by default)

Honest numbers: when something cannot be measured, Vitals shows a dash, never a made-up zero.

Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no telemetry, no analytics. Nothing leaves your computer unless you turn on remote access yourself.

Free and open source (MIT), forever. Available in English and Romanian.

## Restricted capability justification (runFullTrust)

Vitals is a Win32 system monitor and task manager. It needs full trust to read process and performance information from Windows and to end or re-prioritise processes when the user asks it to.

## Notes for certification

Vitals runs as a normal desktop app. No account or sign-in is needed. Remote access (a LAN server for the phone view) is off by default and only starts when the user turns it on in Settings > Remote access. The self-updater, the Task Manager replacement and the optional sensors service are disabled in the Store build.
