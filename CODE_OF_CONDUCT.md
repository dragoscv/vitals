# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not about each other.

## What we expect

- **Be welcoming.** People come here with different backgrounds, hardware and
  levels of experience. A first-time contributor reporting a wrong temperature
  reading is doing valuable work.
- **Be constructive.** "This will break on hybrid CPUs because E-cores report
  differently" is useful. "This is wrong" is not.
- **Assume competence.** If something looks like a mistake, ask why it was
  done that way before assuming it is one. It is often deliberate and the
  reason is often in a comment.
- **Accept that "no" is an answer.** Not every feature belongs in the product.
  A rejected proposal is not a rejection of you.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
sustained disruption, or publishing others' private information. Sexualised
language or imagery has no place here.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems by emailing the maintainers via GitHub. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or ban a
participant, for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

We will explain our reasoning. We will not debate it indefinitely.
